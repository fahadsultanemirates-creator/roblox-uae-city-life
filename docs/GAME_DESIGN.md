# Game design — Roblox UAE – City Life

## Original vision
A colorful, family-friendly Roblox social roleplay world celebrating the UAE. Players can travel, explore, socialize, and drive while keeping their own Roblox avatar and accessories.

## Creator and owner
Roblox username supplied in the discussion: `Muhammadsultan59966`; display name `Muhammadsultan_59966`. A rainbow-styled avatar represents the owner but is **not** a forced avatar for players. The Roblox **publishing account** determines the actual experience owner; an in-game label does not.

## The seven emirates
1. Dubai — Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, skyscrapers and city roads.
2. Abu Dhabi — Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque-inspired landmark and city.
3. Sharjah — heritage, cultural buildings, parks.
4. Ajman — beaches and town.
5. Umm Al Quwain — lagoon and boardwalk.
6. Ras Al Khaimah — Jebel Jais-inspired mountains.
7. Fujairah — fort, mountains and coastline.

Additional future assets: airports, hotels, shopping centres, hospitals, schools, homes, desert safari, museums and more. This is a stylized mini-map, not a precise geographic reconstruction.

## Three vehicle designs
The user supplied photo references; first prototypes use basic Roblox Parts, NOT photo-identical car meshes:
- Police: white SUV with dark-green door panels and a blue lightbar.
- Ambulance: blue/white van with red medical markings and roof emergency lights.
- Fire engine: yellow truck with red detailing, grey compartments and blue lights.

Future goals: responsive steering and physics, wheel animation, sirens, roleplay missions, mobile inputs, refined accurate vehicle shells and decorative markings.

## UAE flags
Exactly **four** flags on buildings, not on every street or pole.

## Game thumbnail
The agreed thumbnail concept shows the rainbow owner avatar, Burj Khalifa, UAE city scene and three emergency vehicles. The concept PNG was shared separately in chat; images have not been installed on Roblox.

## Development roadmap
- **P0**: seven-emirates block map, spawn, 3 vehicles, owner title, welcome UI, local playtest.
- **P1**: improve driving, road routing, collisions, emergency lighting, detailed buildings, visual design.
- **P2**: missions, shops, houses, realistic landmark interiors, multiplayer testing, mobile support, thumbnail upload, polish, public launch.

## Current status
GitHub stores source scripts. The prototype has **not** been playtested in Roblox Studio or published to an account. Use the provided generator in Roblox Studio to create and test the map. Do not share Roblox credentials.
